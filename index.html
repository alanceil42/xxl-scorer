      Searching the list

      The search field takes a name or the game's own search terms, in English, so most of what works in the game's storage works here too.

      cranidos
      name or form containing it; case and accents don't matter
      74 1-151 387-
      Pokédex number or range
      cp700-800 cp-500 hp100-
      CP or HP: one value, a range, or open at one end
      xxl xxs lucky
      XXL, XXS, lucky Pokémon
      rock fire
      type
      4* 3-4*
      appraisal stars from the IV sum: 4* = 45, 3* = 37-44, 2* = 30-36, 1* = 23-29, 0* = 0-22
      4attack 0-1defense 3-4hp
      one IV in the game's groups: 0 = 0, 1 = 1-5, 2 = 6-10, 3 = 11-14, 4 = 15
      year2024 year2020-2022
      catch year
      score1100-
      showcase points (not in the game)
      check
      rows marked to check (not in the game)

      Combine: , or · & or a space: and · ! not. Or goes first, as in the game: fire,water&xxl is fire or water, and XXL.

      Which screenshots to take

      Two screenshots of each Pokémon's page in the game, one with the appraisal and one without. Screenshots of the same Pokémon become one row.

      • Many at once is fine: swipe through your storage, once with the appraisal and once without.
      • Whole screenshots, not cropped or edited. The game in English (other languages are not supported yet).
      • Nothing is uploaded: screenshots are read on this device and dropped.
